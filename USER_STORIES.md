# Firstdose — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Hub case manager

- As a hub case manager, I want my queue ordered by which patient is closest to abandoning, so that my day goes to the cases at risk rather than the ones that shout loudest.
- As a hub case manager, I want every barrier to show its owner and its clock, so that I know whether I am waiting on a payer, a pharmacy or a prescriber's office and exactly when to chase.
- As a hub case manager, I want to raise an appeal with the denial reason and that payer's own criteria already attached, so that the appeal is built once and correctly.
- As a hub case manager, I want a contact to be uncloseable while an adverse event mention is unrouted, so that the safety duty is part of the work rather than a separate discipline I might forget. *(exception path)*

### Patient and caregiver

- As a patient, I want to see where my therapy is stuck and what happens next, so that the silence between prescription and first dose stops feeling like abandonment.
- As a caregiver, I want to schedule injection training and confirm shipment in one place, so that starting therapy is a single process rather than four phone numbers.
- As a patient, I want to withdraw my authorisation and have the programme stop sharing my information without losing the therapy support I still need. *(exception path)*

### Field reimbursement manager

- As a field reimbursement manager, I want to see a prescriber office's stalled cases and the specific missing item on each, so that my visit resolves paperwork rather than discovers it.
- As a field reimbursement manager, I want approval and overturn rates by payer and plan, so that I can tell an office which pathway actually works for that patient's coverage.
- As a field reimbursement manager, I want to be structurally unable to see patient-level data I am not authorised for, so that the firewall protects me as much as it protects the patient.

### Specialty pharmacy intake coordinator

- As a specialty pharmacy coordinator, I want to receive and return case work through a structured exchange without logging into the manufacturer's tool, so that participating costs me no new workflow.
- As a specialty pharmacy coordinator, I want the missing prescription detail named precisely when triage stalls, so that I am not faxing back and forth to discover which field is wrong.
- As a specialty pharmacy coordinator, I want REMS and certification prerequisites checked before the case reaches me, so that I am not the one discovering the product cannot legally be dispensed. *(exception path)*

### Programme compliance and pharmacovigilance officer

- As a pharmacovigilance officer, I want every adverse event from every programme contact routed inside the clock with an auditable trail, so that a safety inspection of the programme is a report run rather than an investigation. *(governance path)*
- As a compliance officer, I want assistance eligibility applied by published rule with every exception logged and approved, so that anti-kickback exposure is demonstrably controlled rather than argued after the fact. *(governance path)*
- As a compliance officer, I want payer collaboration confined to de-identified population outcomes under the signed data-use agreement, so that the analysis the partnership promised cannot drift into individual-level sharing. *(governance path)*
